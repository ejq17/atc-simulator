## Approximate elements (summary)

- ALL runway holding positions (derived at a fixed 250 ft; no source data).
- Parking: airline stands and three GA stands are real OSM ways; FBO / north-ramp GA spots are synthetic grid points inside a real apron polygon; no cargo ramp.
- Taxiway geometry in general: OSM centrelines, positional accuracy beyond the runway thresholds not assessed (OSM runway endpoints were not compared with NASR; the runways come from NASR).
- Unnamed lanes (59 ways + 0 excluded OSM-only ref): flagged approximate on every edge.
- Runway-to-taxiway connection nodes: OSM ways end on the runway centreline (within 1.5 m of it), which this build snaps exactly onto the centreline; real fillets/entrances are wider.
- Vertices within 3 m of another way were merged / snapped; edges shorter than 1.5 m were contracted.
- Taxiway names inferred for untagged ways by continuity (table above); no diagram label georeferencing for this airport.
- Runway 09/27 (2636 x 60 ft, NASR) is NOT in the built airport: no Part 121 / 380 use, takeoff not authorised (airport authority request) and restricted to aircraft under 6000 lb. Taxiways E, E1-E3 and G1-G3 that serve it remain as geometry.
- Displaced threshold of runway 15 (271 ft per NASR) is modelled by the simulator (`displacedThresholdFt`).
- No MVA sectors, holding patterns or fix altitudes are included for this airport (not transcribed; never invented).
- The FBO ramp north of runway 9/27 shown on the diagram is not mapped as an apron in OSM: FBO and GA spots are synthetic (north ramp) or the three unreferenced OSM stands (south ramp).
