## Approximate elements (summary)

- ALL runway holding positions (derived at a fixed 250 ft; no source data).
- Parking: stand positions and lead-in lines are real OSM geometry; stand-to-terminal assignment follows the AIP stand charts; aircraft size limits per stand are not modelled.
- Taxiway geometry in general: OSM centrelines, positional accuracy beyond the runway thresholds not assessed (the runways come from the AIP thresholds; pavement ends of 09L and 09R are derived from the displaced thresholds; OSM runway endpoints were not compared).
- Unnamed lanes (19 ways + 147 excluded OSM-only ref): flagged approximate on every edge.
- Runway-to-taxiway connection nodes: OSM ways end on the runway centreline (within 1.5 m of it), which this build snaps exactly onto the centreline; real fillets/entrances are wider.
- Vertices within 3 m of another way were merged / snapped; edges shorter than 1.5 m were contracted.
- Taxiway names inferred for untagged ways by continuity (table above); no diagram label georeferencing for this airport.
- Displaced thresholds of 09L (309 m) and 09R (308 m): `RunwayEnd.threshold` is the DERIVED pavement end, `displacedThresholdFt` moves the landing threshold to the AIP threshold coordinates.
- Taxiway names with a suffix letter (A9E, N4W ...) and the "Link nn" stubs are not named in the graph (parser limit); stands are the OSM parking_position ways whose number is on the AIP stand charts, MARS sub-stands (209L/209R ...) are not separate positions.
- No MVA sectors (the AD 2-EGLL-5-1 ATC surveillance minimum altitude chart is not transcribed).
