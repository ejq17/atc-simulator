## Approximate elements (summary)

- ALL runway holding positions (derived at a fixed 250 ft; no source data).
- ALL parking positions except the 10 airline gate stands; synthetic GA / FBO / FAA / ANG spots and the pad spots.
- Taxiway geometry in general: OSM centrelines, positional accuracy beyond the runway thresholds not assessed (the OSM runway endpoints differ from NASR by ~1 ft at three ends and ~22 ft at the 04 end).
- Unnamed lanes (32 ways + 1 excluded OSM-only ref): flagged approximate on every edge.
- Runway-to-taxiway connection nodes: OSM ways end on the runway centreline (within 1.5 m of it), which this build snaps exactly onto the centreline; real fillets/entrances are wider.
- Vertices within 3 m of another way were merged / snapped; edges shorter than 1.5 m were contracted.
- Taxiway names inferred for untagged ways (table above) and the diagram-label georeferencing (a few pixels, ~10 m).

## Comparison with the FAA airport diagram (00669AD, cycle 2610)

How it was checked: the OSM-derived graph was drawn on the rendered diagram (`data/sources/raw/dtpp_2610_txt/AD-1.png`, georeferenced with the diagram graticule; the diagram is printed rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise, north left / east up) and each identifier was compared with the label positions in `00669AD_bbox.html`. Runways, the 12 identifiers, both runway crossings (A over 13/31, B over 04/22), the HS 1 ellipse, the 22-end and 04-end wraps of A, the B wraps to both 13/31 thresholds, C to the ANG pad, D from 04/22 across A, G at the 04 end, H/J on the terminal side (south-west of 13/31; the task brief's "north-east" was a guess, the diagram and OSM agree on south-west), K at the 31 end, and L/M/N/P around the terminal apron all land on the printed pavement and under the matching labels.

Remaining mismatches and caveats (honest list):

1. **D over-extends.** OSM tags the whole perimeter loop of the ANG ramp (about 950 m of the 1.66 km of edges carrying D) as D. The diagram labels D only on the connector from runway 04/22 across A to the north corner of the ANG ramp; the ramp perimeter itself is apron pavement (the diagram's G label is at the ramp's south corner, where OSM has the short way G, ~123 m). Kept as tagged and listed here; edges around the ramp are not corrected by hand.
2. **G is only a stub.** G does not exist as a ref in OSM; it is assigned by diagram-label proximity to the single short untagged way between the A end near the 04 threshold and the ANG ramp (the label is 20 m from it and it is the closest way). The part of G that the diagram shows continuing along the ramp edge is mapped in OSM as D (see 1).
3. **C and B pad lanes.** The comb of 12-90 m lanes at the ANG arm/dearm pad (13 end) and the ANG hardstand area south-west of runway 13/31 are OSM "taxiway" ways with no identifier. Those that join B or C end-to-end within 20 degrees inherited B/C by the continuity rule; the others stay unnamed. The diagram labels none of them (they are pad/apron lanes), so the inherited C/B on these short stubs is an inference, not a printed fact.
4. **L / M / N apron lanes.** OSM tags only the outer terminal-apron lanes L, M, N, P. The inner apron lanes (around the central island of the terminal apron) are untagged; most of them took L by continuity. The diagram labels only L (north end), M, N, P and shows the rest as apron pavement, so the extra "L" mileage (about 1.8 km of 19 ways) is an inference and approximate.
5. **K / I at the 31 end.** The long lane along the FAA/Coast Guard ramp edge is OSM-tagged "I", which is not an FAA identifier and is not on the diagram; it is kept as an unnamed lane (excluded identifier) because it joins J to K and gives the Coast Guard helipads and the FAA ramp access. Way 802704509 (the loop at the 31-end ANG arm/dearm pad) sits equally close to the B and K labels (10 m vs 8 m); K was chosen by proximity.
6. **Hold lines are not on the diagram or in OSM.** Every hold position is derived at 250 ft from the centreline (see section above). The diagram prints no hold-line coordinates; the real lines vary by taxiway and aircraft design group.
7. **Where the diagram and OSM disagree on pavement.** The OSM B wraps connect to the runway centreline about 66 m from each 13/31 threshold (inside the runway pavement); the diagram draws the wraps meeting the runway ends in the blast pad. Differences of a few tens of metres are within the georeferencing and OSM accuracy and were not corrected.
8. **GA ramp / FBO / FAA ramp / ANG ramp have no taxilane geometry in OSM**; spots there are synthetic and attached to the nearest lane with apron connectors (up to 150 m). The GA ramp (east lobe of the apron polygon) is only reachable through those connectors.
9. **Diagram labels that are not taxiways** (the ATIS-box "D" tab and the "H" of the H1 helipad callout) are ignored automatically because no way lies within 30 m.
