# UK phraseology at London Heathrow (EGLL) versus the simulator's FAA phraseology

The simulator is written to FAA JO 7110.65 / AIM phraseology (docs/FAA-REFERENCES.md). EGLL is the first non-US airport and the UK uses CAA CAP 413
(Radiotelephony Manual) and ICAO-based phraseology. This page lists the differences that matter at Heathrow, which of them are implemented, and which are
not (they are repeated under "Known differences" in the README so nobody mistakes an EGLL session for UK training).

Source: **CAP 413 Radiotelephony Manual, Edition 24 (effective 1 July 2026)**, https://www.caa.co.uk/cap413 (PDF
`https://www.caa.co.uk/publication/download/18165`, downloaded 2026-10-21, text extracted with pdfjs-dist and read; 11,290 lines). Chapter and paragraph
numbers below are those printed in that edition. The CAA page notes Supplementary Instructions (SIs) that amend the manual between editions; the SIs were
not applied here. The UK AIP (AIRAC 10/2026) supplies the Heathrow-specific procedures (AD 2.20, 2.21, 2.22). Nothing below comes from memory of
real-world Heathrow transmissions.

## Differences

| Topic | Simulator (FAA) | UK / Heathrow | In the sim at EGLL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pressure | "altimeter three zero one zero" (inHg) | "QNH one zero one three" in hectopascals; CAP 413 ch. 2 para 2.13 (each digit separately) and Table 5 (QNH 1000 hPa "one thousand"), ch. 4 para 4.16 ("check that the aircraft is in possession of the latest QNH", example "new QNH 1021"); AIP GEN 3.5 para 3.8.10 (Q group, whole hPa, rounded down) | **Implemented**: `Airport.altimeterUnit = 'hPa'` (from the facility JSON), ATIS reads QNH, AI phrase and pilot readback say "QNH ...", the parser accepts "QNH one zero one three" (stored as inHg, compared in whole hPa), top bar and weather drawer show `Q1013`. Not done: "one thousand" / "tousand" forms for round values (QNH 1000 is read digit by digit) |
| Visibility | statute miles ("visibility one zero") | metres below 10 km, "10 km" at or above (ATIS example in CAP 413 ch. 4 para 4.214: "10 km"); RVR in metres (ch. 4 paras 4.199-4.202: "RVR runway 27, 800 metres") | **Implemented** for the ATIS, top bar and weather drawer (`src/weather/units.ts`). RVR is the existing ATIS RVR segment (feet / metres not switched) |
| Wind | "wind two seven zero at one five" | "surface wind 270 20" without unit word (ch. 4 para 4.51, 3.44-3.45: passed at start-up request, at first contact with Approach, on final) | Existing wording kept (knots, digits). The words "surface wind" are not used |
| Runway designator | "runway nine left" (`spokenRunway`) | each digit separately including the leading zero: "runway zero nine left" (ch. 2 para 2.13; designator before the clearance, ch. 4 para 4.35: "Runway 09 left cleared for take-off") | **Not implemented**: the simulator says "nine left" (a per-airport flag was considered and left out because every phrase, readback and parser path uses `spokenRunway`) |
| Line up | "line up and wait" (JO 7110.65 3-9-4) | civil: "line up" / "runway 28 line up" (ch. 4 paras 4.17, 4.20, 4.23: with the holding-point designator when not full length, "via C2 line up runway 28"); "LINE UP AND WAIT" appears only in the military chapter 10 | Existing FAA command ("line up and wait") works; the pilot readback is FAA wording. Not UK-specific |
| Hold | "hold short of runway 27L" | "hold position" for an aircraft already stopped (ch. 4 paras 4.12, 4.38); "hold at Bravo 2" (4.19); "taxi holding point A1 runway 24 via Charlie" (4.12 examples) | Not implemented: "taxi to runway 27R via ..." phrase kept; hold-short and runway-crossing instructions exist as in the FAA model. AIP AD 2.20 para 2 (Runway Crossing Procedure): crossing instructions carry a holding point as clearance limit, then change to the Air controller, then "runway vacated" |
| Crossing | "cross runway 27L" | "cross runway 24 report vacated" (ch. 4 para 4.14-4.15), conditional "behind the landing A320, via Bravo 1 cross runway 26 behind" | The conditional crossing ("behind the landing ...") exists in the parser; "report vacated" is not added |
| Conditional line up | not in JO 7110.65 | "behind the landing DC9, line up Runway 26 behind" (ch. 4 para 4.33; five-part form: callsign, condition, subject, clearance, reiteration); AIP AD 2.20 para 2 gives the Heathrow forms ("behind the departing Sky Train A330, line up Runway 27L behind") | Not implemented for line up (only the crossing form exists) |
| Take-off clearance | "runway 27R, cleared for takeoff" | same shape (ch. 4 para 4.35); "wind" is not passed with it by default | Same wording |
| Landing | "runway 27L, cleared to land" | same, with "surface wind 270 7"; "report final" / "long final" (ch. 4 para 4.51); "continue approach" (4.54) | Existing landing phraseology; "report final" is not generated |
| Vacating | "exit left / right" (AIM) | "vacate left", "when vacated contact Ground 118.350", "runway vacated" (ch. 4 para 4.68) | Pilot reports "clear of runway"; FAA wording |
| Taxi to stand | "taxi to gate ..." | "taxi to Stand 27 via taxiway Alpha" (ch. 4 para 4.68) | Not implemented: the simulator says "gate three zero three" for a stand; stand names read "Stand five zero one" in the pilot start-up call |
| Start-up | clearance delivery gives route and altitude | Heathrow: initial call to Heathrow Delivery with aircraft type, stand number, QNH and ATIS letter (AD 2.20 para 2 Start-up Procedures); pushback and start approved by Ground (A-CDM TOBT/TSAT) | Not implemented; the pilot's first call quotes the ATIS letter only |
| Departure clearance | "cleared to X via the SID, climb via, departure frequency, squawk" | SID name spoken with number and letter ("Modmi one juliet"); "maximum 250 KIAS below FL100" (AD 2.22 para 7); cap "do not climb above 6000 until cleared by ATC" (SID chart warning); after take-off contact London Control (SID charts) | **Implemented (subset)**: the clearance names a SID of the departure runway (`departureProcedureFor`, hash of the callsign, DESIGN VALUE), initial altitude 6000 ft, departure frequency London Control 134.125; the SID route, noise preferential route and 250 KT limit are not flown |
| Altitude vs level | "descend and maintain 3000" | "descend to altitude 2000 feet QNH 1000" / "descend flight level 50" (ch. 3 Level Reporting, examples); transition altitude 6000 ft at Heathrow (AD 2.17, SID and approach charts) | Not implemented: the simulator uses altitudes in feet with "maintain"; the transition altitude is only in the data notes |
| Speed | "reduce speed to 180" | "180 KT on base, 160 KT to 4 DME, 220 KT from the hold" (AD 2.22 para 3 iii) | The existing speed command works; the Heathrow speed profile is not applied by the AI |
| Squawk | "squawk 4321" | "squawk 6402" (ch. 2 para 2.69 example) | Same word |
| Approach unit | "contact Boston Approach" | "Heathrow Director" (AIP AD 2.18 call sign), arrivals report "callsign, distance, type of approach, runway" on transfer to Tower (AD 2.22 para 3) | Facility call names from the data (`RadioFrequency.callName`): Heathrow Delivery / Ground / Tower / Director, London Control. The transfer report is not generated |
| ATIS | "Atlantic City Airport information Alpha, 1651 Zulu. Wind ... Altimeter ... Read back all hold short instructions" | "Stourton Approach Information Alpha. 0850 hours weather. Runway 28. 240 degrees 12 kt. 10 km. ... QNH 1011. Acknowledge receipt of information Alpha on first contact" (CAP 413 ch. 4 para 4.214; elements per CAP 670 COM05); separate departure ATIS 121.935 and arrival ATIS 128.080 (AD 2.18) | **Implemented (subset)**: "Heathrow information Bravo ...", QNH, metres, no hold-short sentence, ends "Acknowledge receipt of information Bravo on first contact." The element order stays the FAA one (wind first); one ATIS for both directions |
| Runway in use | "landing 27L, departing 27R" | segregated mode: one runway lands, the other departs, swapped at 15:00 on westerly operations (operator programme); 27 preferred up to 5 kt tailwind on a dry runway (AD 2.20 para 6) | **Implemented**: `runway_configurations` W-ALT-27R, W-ALT-27L, E-SEG-09L chosen by headwind; the 15:00 swap and the weekly alternation are not automatic (documented in the facility JSON) |
| Holding stacks | not modelled in KACY / KMHT | OCK, BIG, LAM, BNN terminal holds; radar vectors from the stack; "descent clearance includes an estimate of the track distance to touchdown" (AD 2.22 para 3) | Arrivals fly the transcribed STAR to the stack / the ILS feeder fix; vectors, the distance-to-touchdown statement and time-and-level reports are not generated |
| Noise rules | none | CDA, ILS intercept altitudes 2500 / 3000 ft, 4 % climb gradient (AD 2.21) | Data only (facility JSON `noise_and_operating_rules`); not enforced |
| Low visibility | LVP not modelled | LVP when RVR < 600 m, "follow the greens", stop bars (AD 2.20 paras 2-3) | Not modelled |
| Telephony | N-number and airline words | "Speedbird two four six": airline callsign digits one by one (CAP 413 ch. 2 Table 4) | The simulator groups digits ("Speedbird one twenty three", existing FAA convention for airline callsigns) |

## Scope decision

Implemented: pressure and visibility units end to end (weather parsing, ATIS, displays, AI and pilot phrases, controller parsing), the UK ATIS ending,
the Heathrow frequencies and call names, SID naming with the 6,000 ft cap, segregated runway configurations, live weather through the backend. Everything
else in the table marked "not implemented" keeps the FAA behaviour and is listed in the README "Known differences". EGLL in this simulator is not UK ATC
training.
