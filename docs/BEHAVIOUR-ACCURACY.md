1. **T3 (wake interval)**. I wrote that a C172 needs at least 2 minutes behind an A320 under 3-9-6 and cited 5/6 NM radar wake minima for "Category D". My first intersecting-runway measurement placed the 22 follower at the B-1 hold, which is already 500 ft beyond the intersection; the conclusions ("1,079 ft before the intersection", "arrival at 1 nm refused") were an artefact of that placement and of the old rule.
