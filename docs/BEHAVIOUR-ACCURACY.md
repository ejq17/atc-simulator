### 8.2 INACCURATE items, prioritised, with the concrete fix

1. **F9 Rollout and exit (high; blocks the runway and crosses an intersection).** Measured: A320 landing on runway 4 stops on the runway at 5,163 ft after passing through 13/31; C172 occupies runway 13 for 142 s. Cause: `decelKtPerSec` 4.0 (A320) / 3.0 (C172) and `chooseExit` (`flightDynamics.ts:125`) that only accepts exits reachable at that rate, with a fall back to a full stop on the runway. Fix: (a) use a normal landing braking rate of about 6 kt/s (A320, actual landing distance about 2,900 ft in the Airbus chart) and 4.4 kt/s (C172, Textron 575 ft roll at max braking, REALISM-VERIFICATION.md section 1); (b) when no exit is reachable at the normal rate, brake up to the max rate instead of stopping (fail only when even max braking cannot reach any exit); (c) never let the stop point fall beyond an intersecting runway's hold line (cap at the 13/31 hold on runway 4/22); (d) choose the first reachable exit by type so a C172 exits at 2,017 ft (C-1) on 13 instead of rolling to 4,444 ft.
2. **I5 Approach gate and intercept angle (high for a controller-training tool).** Fix in `assessApproach` (`approach.ts:233-259`): gate distance = max(5 nm, FAF distance + 1 nm); require intercept point >= gate + 2 nm (flag otherwise; allow down to the gate in VFR/ceiling >= MVA+500 and 3 sm, and down to the FAF only when the pilot requested it, which the sim has no way to state, so flag); maximum intercept angle 30 deg when the intercept point is >= 2 nm outside the gate, 20 deg otherwise (5-9-2 TBL 5-9-1). For KACY ILS 13 that is a gate at 5.6 nm and a 7.6 nm minimum intercept distance; today anything >= 4.6 nm is accepted.
3. **I10b RNAV 22 missed approach hold at TUBER (medium).** Transcribe the chart hold into `holdingText` as "TUBER: 6 NM hold, 197 deg inbound" (parser regex `\b(\d{3})\s*(?:DEG|°)?\s*INBOUND\b` in `holding.ts:65` already accepts it) and add an assertion to `missedApproach.test.ts` line 229 on the inbound course. Check the other `... and hold` fixes with no stated course (RNAV 4 JEDOB/SMITS, VOR 4, RNAV 13 Y RODDI) against their plates at the same time; the engine records an assumption for each one that lacks a transcribed course.
4. **I3 Departure frequency (medium).** In `buildIfrClearance` (`ifr.ts:80`) pick the sector from the bearing (magnetic, from the ARP) of the first fix of the filed route: 310-129 -> `DEP-N` 134.25, 130-309 -> `DEP` 124.6 (`kacy-frequencies.json` sectorization_bearings), and let the clearance carry that frequency; the Tower handoff must then use the same frequency id. For KPHL via CYN the clearance should say "departure frequency one three four point two five".
5. **W4 ATIS approach in use (low, quick).** Derive `approachesInUse` in `makeAtis` (`src/ui/store/session.ts:91`) from `airport.procedures`: "ILS runway 13/31 approach" where an ILS exists, otherwise "RNAV (GPS) runway 4/22 approach" (or VOR 4), never "ILS runway 4/22".
