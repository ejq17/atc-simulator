- **Stuck fighter (ANG92078, F-16 flight of two, runway 4). Cause: the rollout, not the formation.** The F16 figures (140 kt touchdown, 5 kt/s braking, all UNVERIFIED design values, unchanged) cannot make taxiway B (3,397 ft) from a touchdown at ~1,640 ft: 140 kt to the 30 kt exit speed in 1,757 ft needs ~8.9 kt/s, the limit is 7 kt/s (5 x 1.4). The only other turnoff of runway 4 is taxiway A at the 22 end, which `chooseExit` never saw because the data has no hold node touching that runway node (the hold line is 250 ft down the taxiway). With no exit, the fallback wanted to stop 60 m short of the 13/31 intersection, which at 140 kt needs 8.4 kt/s: it overshot and stopped IN the intersection (both runways blocked), then called "stopped on runway four, unable to exit". The flight of two changes nothing in `chooseExit` (the formation length is now used for the stop margins only). Fix: (1) turnoffs whose hold line is down the taxiway (forward-leading, within 450 m, no runway node on the way) are a second-choice tier (`runwayExits(..., extended)`), so the F-16 now rolls to A at the far end of runway 4 on 4, 22, 13 and 31 as a single ship or a flight of two; (2) when nothing at all is reachable the plan never stops in an intersection: it stops short of it if it can, else rolls through and stops clear of it. Every ordinary exit choice (C172, A320, B738 ...) is unchanged; the one A320 touchdown point (td 600 m, 130 kt, runway 4) that used to stop on the runway now takes the far A.
- **A stopped aircraft can be moved** (`src/sim/runwayStop.ts`, `towerCommands.ts planStoppedExit`, `commandExecutor.ts planTaxi / planCross`): a TAXI with a route from the runway (the route starts at the runway node ahead of the aircraft, runway travel allowed only on its own runway, back-taxi from the node behind as a last choice), "turn right on Alpha", "continue to the end", "turn right at the next taxiway" (EXIT_RUNWAY plans a route to the turnoff), and the crossing of the intersecting runway ("cross runway 13") now works from a runway node, not only from a hold line. The runway stays occupied until the aircraft is geometrically clear; it stops short of the intersection until crossed; a rejected takeoff stopped on the runway (LINE_UP_AND_WAIT) can be taxied off it (new transition to TAXIING). AI Tower answers the "unable to exit" request with the exit, the crossing when it is safe, and the frequency change (same transmission as after a normal landing); AI Tower also taxis a rejected-takeoff aircraft off the runway (never re-clears it from where it stands; taxi to the ramp when no turnoff is ahead); departures wait on the occupied runway and are cleared once it is clear (tested, no deadlock).

**Free-play jam (2026-10-18)** — `src/ai/sequence.ts`, `src/ai/tower.ts`, `src/ai/ground.ts`, `src/ai/world.ts`, `src/game/traffic.ts`, `src/sim/groundMovement.ts`, `src/sim/commandExecutor.ts`, `src/ai/freePlaySoak.test.ts` (+ `freePlayHarness.ts`)
- Report: "free play at 10 arrivals + 10 departures per hour, maxAircraft 14, special traffic off, jams after about 1 h and slows badly". Reproduced with a 2 h soak (KACY, every AI position, seeds 1-4, pilot realism off and moderate = default hold-short omissions / unfamiliar pilots / 4 % readback errors). Both symptoms were real and independent.
- **Before** (main ab7345d, pilot realism off): seed 1 landed 3 and departed 2 in the whole 2 h (last movement at t = 30 min; 14 aircraft sat in the sim from t = 40 min); seeds 2, 3, 4 last moved at t = 20, 40 and 60 min. 500 SEPARATION_VIOLATION / RUNWAY_INCURSION events in seed 1. Wall clock per sim second rose from 0.9 ms (3 aircraft) to 80 ms (14 aircraft); the 2 h run took 364 s.
- **After** (same seeds, same settings): 13-22 landings and 10-20 departures per 2 h run, counts rising through the second hour in 8 of 8 runs, no 15-minute stretch without a landing or departure (the soak's definition of a stall), longest wait 46 min, 42-74 safety events per run (mostly the 26 m "ground proximity" alerts of queues on the runway exit stubs, a known gap), wall clock per sim second 0.3-7 ms typical and 17 ms in the worst 10-minute row (was 0.9 to 84 ms), the eight 2 h runs in 154 s together (one run was 364 s).
- Root causes, each with its evidence:
  1. **Performance (85 % of the run time)**: `planDeparture` (ai/world.ts) ran several hundred taxi route searches (`simulateRunwayTaxiPick` -> `pickRunwayHold` -> `findTaxiRoute`, `--cpu-prof`: `search` 51 % self, `findTaxiRoute` / `pathfinding` closures 80 %) every second for every READY aircraft that Ground could not release yet (8-50 ms per call, 2-3 aircraft waiting). Cached per (start node, runway ends, distance, closures); `planParking` the same (`parkingRouting`). No engine change, same plans.
  2. **No pattern sequencing** (the operational jam): Tower cleared every pattern aircraft to land from downwind and let all of them turn base on their own, so several reached the final together and went around, over and over (seed 1: 6-9 light aircraft in the pattern for 90 minutes, 0 landings). `ai/sequence.ts` now sequences the pattern (JO 7110.65 3-8-1 EXTEND DOWNWIND / 3-10-3): see src/ai/README.md. Landing clearance only from base or final; Approach no longer treats pattern aircraft as leaders of an airliner; the 3 nm "early go-around" gap is 2 nm between two light aircraft (3-10-3 and the engine's 1.5 nm alert apply, not radar separation).
  3. **Lost pending request**: a pilot who asked for wind and altimeter while waiting for landing clearance lost that request when the numbers were read (`executeCommand` clears `pendingRequest` on every accepted command); after a go-around Tower's VFR re-entry plan needed `pending === 'LANDING'`, so the aircraft flew straight out forever (seed 1: 571 s, 16 km out). Engine: `requestBeforeNumbers` is restored after an `AIRPORT_INFO`; Tower also works a VFR missed approach with no request pending.
  4. **Exit-stub deadlocks on the ground** (all four seeds): arrivals on 22 exit onto taxiway B and must cross 4/22 again. (a) The second arrival stopped between the hold line and the runway, its route starting at the hold line behind it: it never "arrived" at the hold, made no crossing call and blocked the first (head-on, 25 m apart). Engine: a route that starts at a runway hold line the aircraft is within 40 m past counts as holding there (`HOLD_START_SNAP_M`); Ground's crossing branch finds the hold from that far. (b) `findLeader`: aircraft stopped on the same spot, and a stopped aircraft 1.4 m BEHIND the subject (`gapAlongRoute` clamped behind-points to 0), each waited for the other for good. (c) The aircraft nearest the runway must cross first (`aheadInCrossingQueue`). (d) Ground no longer crosses an aircraft toward a departure standing head-on at the far hold line (`opposingTraffic` now sees a stationary aircraft that arrived over the path from the other side).
  5. **Ramp deadlocks**: an arrival taxiing in and a pushed-back departure on the same lane, each waiting for the other. Ground holds a pushback while another aircraft's route passes within 90 m of the stand, and `opposingTraffic` counts a READY / PUSHBACK aircraft standing on the path.
  6. **Spawning**: the Poisson clocks put up to 7 arrivals in 10 minutes into a 14-aircraft sim (a runway shared with departures and crossings can take about one landing every 160 s). Generator flow control (src/game/README.md): an arrival is held back while it would reach the threshold within `ARRIVAL_SLOT_SEC` = 160 s of another arrival, or `MAX_ARRIVALS_IN_FLIGHT` = 6 are airborne; no new departure while `MAX_DEPARTURES_ON_GROUND` = 5 are on the ground ("expect a delay" at the gate). Rates are NOT lowered: the clocks still run at 10 + 10 per hour (x ramp), spawns wait. Departures that waited 90 s at the hold and crossings that waited 30 s get a window the delayable pattern traffic leaves free.
- Tests: `src/ai/freePlaySoak.test.ts` (2 h soak, guarded: `FREEPLAY_SOAK=1`, about 5 minutes for the 8 runs; `FREEPLAY_SEEDS`, `FREEPLAY_REALISM`, `FREEPLAY_REPORT_FILE`), `src/ai/patternSequence.test.ts` (five arrivals, pattern leg geometry, the numbers answer keeps the landing request). The default suite stays fast.
- Not resolved: the longest single waits are still long (up to about 46 minutes for an airline departure whose taxi is held while arrivals use the same lanes, and 7 arrivals queue on the exit stub in seeds 1/3 at the end of a run); a 10-minute window without a landing or departure still happens (the guarded soak's stall is 15 minutes). ## Known issues
- The microphone path is proven in automation for all three providers (fake audio device). Not exercised: a physical headset with a human voice, and Deepgram's own recognition accuracy (the client protocol is proven against a mock; a real key is needed for accuracy). Google's Web Speech service needs 1–3 s to open its stream, so speakers should pause a beat after keying; the app cannot buffer for it.
- Whisper's first use downloads ~40 MB of model files from huggingface.co and the ONNX runtime from a CDN; decoding adds ~1.5 s after key release.
- A session without a Ground position strands landed aircraft at the runway exit (pilots told to contact an unworked frequency wait there); choose a position set that includes Ground, or Tower + Ground.
- Browser TTS cannot be routed through WebAudio, so the radio filter applies only to clicks/static, not the pilot voice. The **Local neural** engine fixes that, with caveats: nobody has listened to it (checked by Whisper round trips, level and timing measurements only); WebGPU needs a 326 MB download (fp16 and 4-bit variants produced garbage on WebGPU, measured; 8-bit on WebGPU was 10x slower); the CPU path uses Piper (79 MB), whose voice set is the 9 speakers picked from libritts_r, not Kokoro's. Model files are cached in the Cache API (verified in persistent Chrome; private windows or blocked storage re-download).
- Pilot audio is verified through the browser's own utterance events (`window.__kacyTts`), because speech synthesis output cannot be recorded; a person has not listened. A browser with no speech voices shows "no voices loaded" and pilots are text only. In one of about 15 real-Chrome runs the first readback never started within 15 s (cause not established, it did not recur in 5 repeated and 4 fresh-browser runs; the test now allows 45 s, and `errors` / `outcome` in `window.__kacyTts` would show a `timeout` or `error:*`).
- The strip board is a stacked rack of bays in the 400 px side column (scrolling), not a wide multi-column board; with four AI bays and eight aircraft it needs scrolling. Equipment suffixes on strips (`/G`, `/L`) are simulator assumptions. `LAHSO 13` shows the hold-short runway, `H/S 13` the runway held short of.
- Playwright against the dev server resets when another session edits `src/` (Vite full reload); run it against a build: `E2E_PREVIEW=1 E2E_PORT=5174` (see `src/ui/README.md` Tests).
- Career step 1 completes as soon as the takeoff clearance is accepted.
- Holding-position locations are derived (250 ft from centreline), not charted; OSM taxiway accuracy beyond runway endpoints is unassessed.
- Hot-spot text in the d-TPP ("Rwy 13-31 at Twy A and Rwy 04-22 at Twy B") is reproduced as published; the app marks HS 1 at the runway intersection as drawn on the diagram.
- Lint: 3 non-blocking warnings.

## Ground operations (task/ground-ops)
- Pushback with motion (`src/sim/pushback.ts`, `stepPushback`): airliners / cargo jets on airline and cargo stands ask for pushback, are pushed backwards along the stand lead-in to the apron lane at 1.2 m/s (design value) with the nose swinging onto a lane direction ("tail east" / "face south", non-standard), then call ready to taxi after a seeded interval; GA aircraft start on their own. The engine's spacing logic sees the pushing aircraft; Surface and Tower views draw it moving backwards (interpolated position, heading unchanged). Measured on all 31 real KACY / KMHT stands (docs/BEHAVIOUR-ACCURACY.md G10).
- Engine start timing, hold-short readback requirement (seeded omission, stop at the first hold line, `READ_BACK_HOLD`, 20 s charge, 2-4-3), wrong-taxiway readback error, route-less taxi flagged / refused / "say route", progressive taxi (unfamiliar pilots, segments, AI Ground steps), per-aircraft taxi speed and smooth corner speed, arrivals linger 120 s at the stand.
- Open: pushback speed, start intervals, omission fraction, unfamiliar fraction, 0.85 speed floor and the 120 s linger are design values; no airline FCOM taxi-speed or spacing source was found (AC 150/5300-13B gives only the 13 kt taxilane / 13-30 kt taxiway ranges); the pushback and "say route" phrases have no FAA basis; behaviour was exercised by unit tests and engine runs, not by watching the Surface / Tower views in a browser.

## Imperfect pilots (task/imperfect-pilots)
- Pilot realism levels off / light / moderate / heavy (`src/sim/pilotBehaviour.ts`: one tuning object, every value a design value; per-level table in README). Training lessons off, career steps 1-2 off / 3-5 light / 6-9 moderate / 10-12 heavy, free play selector (default moderate), `Difficulty` mapping easy / normal / hard = light / moderate / heavy for anything else. Levels also set the ground task's `holdShortOmitFraction`, `taxiwayErrorShare`, `unfamiliarFraction`.
- Readback errors: altitude, frequency, squawk (also in IFR clearances), pattern misunderstood, no readback, wrong-callsign answer from a similar callsign, on top of the digit, hold-short and wrong-taxiway errors; correction by re-issue or "did you copy"; the 20 s window starts when the readback is heard; a frequency / IFR-clearance error holds the frequency change / taxi call.
- Timing and discipline: seeded response delays by crew, missed calls, "say again" on long transmissions, blocked transmissions decided in the engine (`RADIO_BLOCKED`, `blocked` to the voice layer, WebAudio squeal, "[blocked]" in the transcript), retries at different delays.
- Callsigns and styles: "November" / type word, abbreviated callsigns after the controller (AIM 4-2-4), "student pilot", airline "with you" / "with Bravo" (non-standard), students (STU marker, slower, progressive taxi, pattern mistakes).
- Requests: runway change, short approach, option, straight-in, stay in the pattern, need a minute, 360, higher / lower, direct, visual, taxi back; AI answers (`src/ai/requests.ts`); new commands `DID_YOU_COPY`, `UNABLE`.
- An uncorrected readback error is flown after the 20 s window (`flyMisread`, `readbackConsequence.test.ts`); takeoff / line up / crossing / landing errors at once. Known limitations: an IFR-clearance frequency error is stored but the DEP handoff still uses the controller's frequency; a wrong-callsign answerer is flown but the addressed aircraft also carries out the clearance; `could not fly` (a query) leaves the aircraft as it was.
- Known limitations kept as they are: the strip request badge shows a request whose call was blocked; the parser's "unable" swallows the rest of the transmission ("unable, runway one three cleared to land" is not read as a clearance); the request wording was not checked against a pilot source; "listen before keying" is not an FAA paragraph read here; the AI controllers never abbreviate callsigns; bench measurements of request frequencies in a played session are missing (R13).

## Vehicle spacing (task/vehicle-spacing)
