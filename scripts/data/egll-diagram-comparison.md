# EGLL: OSM taxiway identifiers versus the UK AIP aerodrome chart (AD 2-EGLL-2-1 / 2-2, AIRAC 10/2026)

Generated by hand from `egll-procedures.json` (`airport_diagram.taxiway_identifiers`), the OSM extract (retrieved 2026-10-21) and the built graph.

* Chart identifiers read (text layer of AD 2-EGLL-2-1 and 2-2, AD 2.8): 69: A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, K, L, M, R, S, T, V, W, Y, Z, A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9E, A9W, A10E, A10W, A11, A12, A13, B1, C1, C2, D1, D2, E1, E2, F1, F2, K1, K2, L1, L2, M1, N1, N2E, N2W, N3, N4E, N4W, N5E, N5W, N6, N7, N8, N10, N11, S1, S3, S4E, S4W, S5E, S5W, S6, S7, S11. Stub taxiways are "Link nn" (labels read: 08 10 11 13 21 22 23 25 26 27 28 31 35 36 41 42 43 44 51 52 53 54 56 57 58).
* OSM `ref` values on taxiway ways: 86 distinct. Main taxiways are tagged "Taxiway A" ... "Taxiway Z"; exits "A1", "N10", "S7"; stubs "Link 41". The profile maps "Taxiway X" to "X" (`osmRef` hook).
* All 18 main taxiways of AD 2.8 (A B C D E F G H K L M R S T V W Y Z) exist in OSM with a ref and are named in the graph. OSM has no ref for taxiway "X" or "U" on the chart (none printed).
* Identifiers the simulator can name (letter plus up to two digits, command parser limit): 55. Present in the built graph: 41. Not in the graph: B1, C1, C2, D1, D2, E1, E2, F1, F2, K1, K2, L1, L2, M1 (apron-side labels such as B1, C1, D1 ... have no OSM way with that ref).
* Not named by design (suffix letter or "Link"): A9E A9W A10E A10W N2E N2W N4E N4W N5E N5W S4E S4W S5E S5W and all "Link nn". Their geometry is kept; untagged ways take the identifier of the way they continue.
* OSM-only identifiers: none beyond the Link stubs (all OSM refs are on the chart).
* Hot spots HS1-HS5 are chart labels with no OSM counterpart; no position is georeferenced.
* Holding positions: OSM has 288 `holding_position` nodes (refs such as N5W, S7, NB2E); the build derives its own hold lines 250 ft from each runway centreline and does not use them as the source.
* Stands: OSM `parking_position` ways with a number on the AIP stand charts (195 used); see `scripts/airports/egll.ts`.
