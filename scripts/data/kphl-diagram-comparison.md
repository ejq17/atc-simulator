## Comparison with the FAA airport diagram (00320AD.PDF, cycle 2610)

Diagram taxiway identifiers (read visually from a 450 dpi render; the page is printed rotated, the text layer splits the rotated labels into single characters): A, A1, A2, A3, B, D, D1, D2, D4, E, E1, E2, E3, E4, E5, F, G, H, J, J1, K, K1, K2, K3, K4, K5, K6, K7, K8, L, M, N, P, P2, P3, P4, P5, P6, Q, R, S, S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, S7, S8, S11, S12, S13, SS, SS1, SS3, T, TA, U, V, W, Y, Z (62). The circled A4 / A5 / A symbols on the diagram are not taxiways (they stand on the extended runway centrelines far beyond the pavement ends) and the circled P symbols are PAPI/VGSI symbols. The inset (west apron / deicing area) names numbered spots 1N...4S, which OSM maps as `aeroway=taxilane` features with those refs.

1. **On the diagram but absent from OSM (never created): K2, K3, P6, S12, S13.** No OSM way carries these refs, and without georeferenced diagram labels the untagged OSM ways could not be assigned to them, so they are missing from the graph.
2. **A1 is on the diagram and in OSM, but excluded from the network.** OSM maps A1 as a closed loop around the end of runway 26 whose nearest neighbouring taxiway point is about 500 m away (taxiway A is not mapped over the last stretch to the 26 end); keeping it would leave the runway-26 holds isolated from the taxi network. The way (way/145778731) is listed in `profile.excludeOsmWays`, reported by the build, and the one validation that the exclusion breaks (end 26 of runway 8/26 has fewer than two entry/exit nodes) is a documented known source gap (`profile.knownGaps`).
3. **In OSM but not on the diagram: S9, S10 and "8"** (one way each). Kept as geometry, identifier excluded (`excludedOsmRefs`); S9/S10 are short apron-lane ways at the west end, "8" is a connector between terminal-side ways.
4. **Untagged ways** (244 of the 325 OSM taxiway ways): 20 are short turn-on fillets collapsed by the build; 200 took the identifier of the named way they continue (continuity rule, at most 20 deg off a straight continuation, iterated), 21 stay unnamed. Many named-by-continuity ways are short ramp or fillet connectors, so their identifier is an inference (the build report lists each decision). J (58 ways) and K (54 ways) collect many such ways: the long-looping apron lanes around the terminals are not separable by the continuity rule.
5. **Runway entrances are mapped as fillets.** As at KMHT, OSM draws the straight crossing taxiway plus short untagged curves; the build collapses them and derives each hold line 250 ft from the centreline on the through taxiway. OSM has **no `holding_position` nodes** at KPHL (0 in the extract), so the derived lines could not be compared with mapped ones.
6. **Displaced threshold.** NASR lists 27L with a displaced threshold of 1913 ft (LDA 9912 ft; the 27L plate "Rwy Ldg 9912"); the simulator models it through `displacedThresholdFt` and the pavement ends stay the runway-end nodes. OSM maps only four runway ways with `ref` (one per runway; 17/35 twice), which the build ignores (runways come from NASR).
7. **Gates.** OSM `gate` nodes (127, refs A2-A26, B1-B16, C17-C31, D1-D16, E1-E17, F1-F39) sit inside the terminal at level 2; the aircraft stands are the OSM `parking_position` ways (lead-in line lane -> stand) with the same ref plus suffix variants (A9A/B, A11A/B, D8A/B, D10A/B, D12A/B, D14A/B, D11L/W, D1L/C/R). The stands used are those inside a terminal apron polygon whose lead-in start lies within 100 m of the mapped taxi/apron network: OSM has no taxilane network on the terminal aprons (34 `taxilane` features exist, mostly the inset spots), so stands deeper in the aprons cannot be connected and are left unused; the remaining connectors are straight apron connectors.
8. OSM runway tags (widths 46 / 61 m, `surface=paved`) are not used; runway geometry, headings and elevations are NASR.
