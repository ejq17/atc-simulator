## Comparison with the FAA airport diagram (00246AD.PDF, cycle 2610)

Diagram taxiway identifiers (read from the chart text and a render): A, A1, A2, A3, A5, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, J1, K, K1, K2, L, M, M1, N, Z (22). The lone "P" words on the chart are PAPI symbols, not a taxiway.

1. **On the diagram but absent from the OSM refs: A5, C, K2.** No OSM way carries these refs, and without georeferenced diagram labels the untagged OSM ways could not be assigned to them, so they are missing from the graph (their geometry, where mapped, is in the unnamed lanes).
2. **In OSM but not on the diagram: D1** (two ways). Kept as geometry, identifier excluded (`excludedOsmRefs`).
3. **Untagged ways** (30): 10 are short turn-on fillets collapsed by the build (see 4); 13 took the identifier of the named way they continue (continuity rule, at most 20 deg off a straight continuation); 7 stay unnamed. Several named-by-continuity ways are short ramp/fillet connectors, so their identifier is an inference, flagged in the table above.
4. **Runway entrances are mapped as fillets.** At most crossings OSM draws the straight crossing taxiway plus short untagged curves from it to the runway centreline on either side. The build collapses those curves when the through taxiway itself reaches the runway (10 ways), places each hold line 250 ft from the centreline on the through taxiway, and removes the degree-2 OSM vertices between a runway node and its hold line from the routing graph so that the hold is adjacent to the runway node (the engine's rollout exit and runway entry use that adjacency).
5. **OSM `holding_position` nodes versus the derived lines.** 26 OSM nodes exist (several without a runway tag, several tagged only by name or ref). On runway 17/35 ten of the eleven derived hold lines fall within 0-1.9 m of an OSM holding_position node (the exception, K1, has no tagged OSM node near it); on 6/24 the agreement is weaker (M 0 m, Z 2 m, A 7 m, K 7-10 m, J1 10 m, J 32 m, M1 68 m) because OSM maps those lines elsewhere or not at all. This supports the 250 ft simplification on 17/35; the lines are still derived, not copied (table "Derived hold lines vs OSM holding_position nodes").
6. **Displaced thresholds** (6: 442 ft, 17: 336 ft, 35: 850 ft per NASR) are in the data (`displacedThresholdFt`) but the runway-end nodes are the pavement ends. OSM also maps the displaced thresholds as separate `runway=displaced_threshold` ways, which the build ignores (runways come from NASR).
7. **Gates.** OSM `gate` nodes are inside the terminal at level 2; the aircraft stands are the OSM `parking_position` nodes of the same ref (1-12, 14, 15; there is no gate 13 in OSM).
8. OSM aerodrome and runway tags (width 46 m, lengths 2332 / 2819 as rounded metres) are not used; runway geometry, headings and elevations are NASR.
